# Demo zones (Plzeň compact layout)

For the testnet demo, `DEMO_ZONES=plzen-compact` (one line in
`deploy/questlayer-hackathon.service`) makes everything that makes or shows an
on-chain transaction reachable in a short walk from náměstí Republiky, where
the demo starts.

## What changes

1. **No zone countdown.** All six zones, the epic and legendary ones too, are
   open the whole local day (00:00 to 24:00 Europe/Prague). Without the flag
   tier 3 opens for 1 h from 18:00 and one tier 2 slot for 3 h from 12:00.
2. **Zones on the ring of parks around the square** instead of across the
   city (normally 2 to 4 km out). The layout lives in
   `server/data/demo-zones/plzen-compact.json`, generated by
   `node scripts/build-demo-zones.mjs` from OpenStreetMap places and the
   CityPack's mapped paths. Tiers (1, 1, 1, 2, 2, 3) still rotate daily.

   | Zone | From the square | Shape |
   |---|---|---|
   | náměstí Republiky | in the square (start) | 85 m circle |
   | sady Pětatřicátníků North | ~290 m NW | 55 m circle |
   | sady Pětatřicátníků West | ~250 m W | 55 m circle |
   | Smetanovy sady | ~330 m SW | 55 m circle |
   | Šafaříkovy sady | ~300 m SE | 55 m circle |
   | sady 5. května | ~330 m E | 75 m circle |

   Zones keep at least 80 m between them (250 m in the citywide layout). The
   round loop through all six is about 2 km.
3. **Guardians, chests and Auras follow the zones**: every zone chest (Aura
   NFT) and its guardian is inside one of these circles.
4. **NPCs and monsters stay near the centre**: walking actors keep within
   250 m of their home, and seeded homes (roamers, the night wisp) lie within
   400 m of the square. The trader (Intents trades), the Gate Keeper (vault),
   the pilgrim's errands and the boss on the square were already central.

Map coins (QGOLD payouts) are denser within 300 m of a zone, so the centre
also gets more coins. Place badges for the cathedral, the synagogue and the
brewery gate and the test-seed creator content were already within ~800 m.

A compact day is its own world (`…:v2-compact`) and plan (its source hash
differs), so switching the flag never mixes zones inside one stored world.
Turning it on mid-day starts a fresh world for that day.

## Switching back for the final product

Delete the `Environment=DEMO_ZONES=plzen-compact` line (or set
`DEMO_ZONES=off`) and deploy. The next world uses the citywide zones with
their rarity windows. `/api/health` shows `demoZones: "off"`. Mainnet refuses
to start with the flag.

## Start where you are (11 Nov 2026)

The compact layout only changes Plzeň. Everywhere else the GPS world is the
normal one: POI quests, funded cell zones and backed coins of the day's map
plan for every street cell (`ensureWorld` in `server/world.mjs`; the plan
covers Czechia for now).

The game starts in that world (`src/play-here.ts`): after sign-in it asks for
location and loads the player's own cell right away. The Plzeň city demo is the
fallback when location is refused or unavailable, and when the player picks
Demo in Map settings (`questlayer.playMode=demo` in localStorage, kept until
they pick Use GPS again). A refused start-up request shows no warning.
