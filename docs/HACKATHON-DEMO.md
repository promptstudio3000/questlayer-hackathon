# QuestLayer demo runbook

## Story

QuestLayer turns a walk into a solo adventure: small street discoveries, changing park locations and collectible character effects. A player can enjoy the first loop before owning an original Aavegotchi. NEAR becomes useful when the earned item becomes a wallet-owned NFT, when that NFT is traded through actual escrow, and when the player chooses a portable public explorer profile.

The presentation must clearly identify the city demo, testnet and simulated GOLD economy. Do not describe the current reward examples as financed by realized native staking income. Do not claim original-art ownership, complete iOS acceptance, canonical Intents execution or hackathon eligibility/submission without their separate evidence.

## Short live path

1. Open the public application and enter or restore a demo explorer. Show the six-sector city overview, then return to the character. The clearly labelled city demo permits moving by clicking; it is not proof of physical GPS presence.
2. Collect a small street coin and open a free park activity. Choose a chest or the ordered expedition. If this explorer has already completed it today, choose another available activity; do not reset the published world for the presentation.
3. Show the confirmed discovery, use `Try it on`, and open Inventory. Display above/below Aura composition and the optional 3D dressing room. Explain that the map uses real OpenStreetMap geography and the player can play alone.
4. Connect a supported NEAR wallet on testnet. The wallet proves account ownership; the app never requests its seed. From an earned Aura, approve its bounded NFT-storage transaction and show confirmed native ownership. Existing recorded proof can be used if a live approval is unavailable, but label it as recorded evidence.
5. Open the native section of Market. Show an actual offer, its current escrow ownership and native test-NEAR price. A complete live trade needs separately controlled seller/buyer testnet wallets and their approvals. The recorded purchase and seller withdrawal are genuine chain evidence; a mock layout fixture is not a trade.
6. In Inventory, review the public alias, selected account, exact owned avatar reference and storage attachment. Tick explicit public consent and approve the NEAR Social publication. Existing SocialDB credit reduces the attachment to one yoctoNEAR. This publishes no GPS, balance or private inventory.
7. Show Game fund: separate coin/zone backing, 50/50 incoming contributions and unclaimed rollover. Explicitly keep its demo status visible. Describe the native staking and financial-review gate as the next major implementation step.
