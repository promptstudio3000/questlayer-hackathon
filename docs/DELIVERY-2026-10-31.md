## City, rewards and presentation

The Plzen CityPack records nineteen candidate park footprints across six sectors and a bounded snapshot of 3,500 public pedestrian ways. Each city/day uses six varied, non-overlapping footprints with at least 250 metres between zone boundaries. Geometry, tiers and activities vary by day. New blueprints use three free zones, two middle-tier zones and one top-tier zone, with four all-day windows plus a three-hour and one-hour window. Published manifests remain stable for their day rather than being silently regenerated.

Each zone has a visit, a chest and an ordered three-point expedition. Completion and XP are personal and durable; shared monetary rewards are won once. Six thousand shared street coins have small 0.01 GOLD demo rewards. Coin and zone backing use separate 50/50 buckets; expired unclaimed backing returns to its own bucket. The approved future creator fee is not activated.

World preparation is durable and DST-aware. Leases, retries, restart recovery and prepared blueprints cover today and tomorrow without reserving financial backing early. Opening an actual world expires old rewards before publication. Straight-line distances are labelled; no walking-route or physical-safety acceptance is claimed.

The map includes a following camera, city overview, globe transition, large character markers and above/below Aura styling. Short visual moves interpolate for 450 ms, cancel safely on a newer position and respect reduced-motion mode. Server eligibility continues to use the actual location fix. Long demo jumps snap.

The optional 3D dressing room downloads only the selected model, verifies its digest and uses bounded proxy/cache limits. The starter model works directly. An observed equipped reference was reduced from about 6.1 MB to 2.0 MB by bounded texture work while preserving geometry. No full artwork catalog is mirrored. The reference preview is not a claim that this project's wallet owns the reference NFT.
