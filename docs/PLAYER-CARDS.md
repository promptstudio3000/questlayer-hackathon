# Aavegotchi wearables, explorer cards and waves

Built 4 November 2026. Code: `server/gotchi-wearables.mjs`, `server/player-waves.mjs`,
`src/GotchiWardrobe.tsx`, `src/PlayerCard.tsx`, `src/wearables.css`. Tests:
`tests/gotchi-wearables.test.mjs`.

## Player problem

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aavegotchi wearables only appeared as a read-only list after linking a Base wallet. Nobody saw one on the map. | 301 official wearables can be found on the map as free in-game clones and worn on the character. | Wearables are the most recognisable Aavegotchi collectible; the map should show them. |

## Wearable clones

- Catalogue: `server/data/gotchi-wearables.json`, generated by
  `node scripts/build-gotchi-wearables.mjs` from `getItemType(itemId)` on the
  Aavegotchi Diamond on Base (`0xA99c…9dBF`). Names, slot, rarity and the item's
  x/y/width/height on the 64×64 gotchi canvas. Background wearables are left out.
- Artwork is never copied into the repo. `GET /api/wearables/art/:id.svg`
  (public, catalogue ids only, 240 per minute per IP) reads `getItemSvg(itemId)`
  from the contract through the bounded `GotchiItemArt` cache (400 items, 8 MB,
  24 h) and serves it with a sandboxing CSP.
- Finds: about one street find in three becomes a wearable find (at most 12 per
  world), chosen deterministically from the coin id. Rarity weights: common 40 %,
  uncommon 26 %, rare 18 %, legendary 10 %, mythical 5 %, godlike 1 %. The map draws
  the real item in a rarity ring; tapping it heads there. Picking it up goes
  through the normal street coin claim (GPS check, travel guard, XP caps) and adds
  the clone. A second copy of the same item is reported as a duplicate.
- A clone is a cosmetic database row (`wearable_clones`). It is not an NFT, not
  tradable and has no GOLD value. Nothing about rewards, XP or the economy changed.
- Wearing: one item per slot (head, face, eyes, body, hand, pet). The starter
  Aavegotchi on the map is drawn as an SVG with each worn item at its official
  canvas place, in the contract's layer order. An original Aavegotchi skin keeps
  its own on-chain wearables instead.

## Verified originals

- A player links a Base address with a signed message (Wardrobe → Link wallet,
  or MetaMask/Rabby sign-in from PR #172, which links the same address).
- `GET /api/aavegotchi` reads `itemBalances` and each gotchi's items on Base and
  stores the snapshot in `external_links`.
- Any catalogued item in a snapshot younger than 24 hours is verified. Owned
  originals join the wardrobe automatically, already verified. After 24 hours the
  gold check disappears until the player checks again; the clone stays.
- Public data never includes the wallet address: other players see only
  "verified" per item.

## Explorer card

`GET /api/players/card?playerId=` (60 per minute). Available only while the
other explorer shares their position (the existing 15-minute opt-in) and neither
side blocked the other. It returns name, level, worn wearables with verified
flags and Aura colours. No address, account, inventory, balance or history.
The card offers waves and Block (the existing bilateral block from W10, which
also hides presence, notes and waves).

## Messages: what is built and what is proposed

**Built now: waves.** Six ready phrases ("Hello!", "Nice look!", "Want to
explore together?", "There is a find near here.", "Thanks!", "See you around!").

- No free text, so nothing to moderate and no way to pass phone numbers, links,
  addresses or other personal data.
- Both explorers must be sharing their position right now, in the same realm
  (demo or GPS), with no block either way.
- Limits: one wave per pair per minute, 20 sent per hour, 30 received per hour.
- Delivered through the existing presence poll (every 8 s) as a toast, read once,
  deleted after an hour. A block drops unread waves.

**Proposed next (not built): short chat between explorers who waved back.**

1. Opens only after both sides waved at each other (mutual consent).
2. Text up to 200 characters, links and digit runs (phone numbers) refused,
   the same NEAR AI / Ollama safety filter as quest texts screens each message.
3. Report button with the W10 report flow; three reports from different
   players mute the sender until an operator reviews.
4. No photos, no location in messages, history kept 24 hours.
5. Under-age safety: chat stays off for accounts younger than a day and can be
   switched off entirely in Settings.
